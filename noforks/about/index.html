Bacliff · Grand Avenue

From food truck to Bacliff restaurant

No Forks cooked as a food truck in Bacliff, then opened a storefront at 523 Grand Ave. The menu is smash burgers, wings, and premium sides. Guests already talk about the Popper, the Sweet Heat, garlic parmesan tots, and house ranch.

No Forks is owned by Chanse Slaten and Cloe Slatne. They carried the restaurant from its food-truck chapter into the current Grand Avenue storefront.

Today, you’ll find No Forks at 523 Grand Ave in Bacliff, serving the same straightforward food with a permanent front door and the same hands-on local personality.

Where it started

The original trailer is part of the story, not the limit of the brand. Chanse and Cloe carried the same hands-on spirit into the Grand Avenue restaurant.
