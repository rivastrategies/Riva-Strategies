From a written agreement to closing

Understand the documents and questions to organize between a written property agreement and closing in Texas, including title review, settlement figures, and possession.

By Lone Star Property Solutions · Updated November 9, 2026

The useful starting point

Begin with the actual agreement and the professionals handling the transaction. Record requested documents, responsible contacts, and agreement-specific dates. Closing, funding, and possession arrangements should each be confirmed rather than assumed.

In this guide1. Establish the working checklist2. Gather available property documents3. Review title and outstanding items4. Confirm figures and handover arrangements

1. Establish the working checklist

Keep one list with five columns: item, requested by, needed by, status, and question to resolve. Ask the responsible professional to confirm the requirements for your transaction. An online property inquiry is an introduction and does not begin a contract or title process by itself.

  • Identify the agreed contacts and how you will verify messages from them.
  • Record the dates and conditions in your agreement and any addenda.
  • Keep preferred timing separate from confirmed contractual dates.

2. Gather available property documents

Start with records you already have, then respond to requests from the title or other responsible professional. An existing survey may be useful, but its acceptance and any additional requirements must be confirmed for the transaction.

  • Available survey and related records
  • Relevant leases or occupancy arrangements
  • Repair records, permits, and available association information
  • Requested ownership or authority documents through the professional’s secure process

3. Review title and outstanding items

A title commitment comes before closing and identifies requirements and possible exceptions. The title policy follows. Ask the title professional to explain outstanding items and what needs to happen before the transaction can proceed.

  • Who is responsible for resolving each open item?
  • Which documents or approvals remain outstanding?
  • Do the available records match the property and transaction being discussed?

4. Confirm figures and handover arrangements

Review the settlement figures and ask about unfamiliar charges or changes. Confirm signing, funding, possession, and key handover arrangements separately. Use contact information you independently know to verify payment instructions; an unexpected email alone should not change where funds are sent.

  • Keep sensitive identity, banking, and payoff documents out of the public property submission form.
  • Use the closing professional’s approved secure process for sensitive information.
  • Resolve questions about final documents with the appropriate professional before relying on them.
