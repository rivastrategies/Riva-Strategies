Behind on Mortgage Payments? Prepare Your Next Steps

A southeast Houston homeowner checklist for missed mortgage payments, lender conversations, housing counseling, and evaluating a possible as-is property sale.

By Lone Star Property Solutions · Updated November 9, 2026

The useful starting point

Contact your mortgage servicer using independently verified details and ask what options apply to your account. If selling is one option you want to explore, gather property facts and compare written terms while continuing the conversations needed to address the loan.

In this guide1. Start with the account, not an assumption2. Locate housing-help resources3. Compare a sale against your goals4. Share enough for an initial local review

1. Start with the account, not an assumption

Missed payments do not tell a buyer everything about your account or any pending action. Ask your servicer to explain the current status and documents it needs. Keep a record of your conversations and outstanding questions.

  • Use a known statement or verified website to find contact details.
  • Ask for an explanation of available options and next steps.
  • Keep notices and responses together for your advisers.

2. Locate housing-help resources

The CFPB provides mortgage-help information, and Texas housing resources point homeowners toward foreclosure-prevention counseling. These services have a different role from a property buyer. Use them to understand your options rather than assuming a sale is the only path.

  • Ask about HUD-approved housing counseling.
  • Confirm who you are dealing with before sharing account details.
  • Discuss time-sensitive correspondence promptly with appropriate advisers.

3. Compare a sale against your goals

What matters is whether a proposed transaction fits your circumstances after costs, payoffs, conditions, and timing are considered. Compare a direct purchase with other feasible approaches using actual written information.

  • Keep uncertain amounts marked as unknown.
  • Ask a closing professional to confirm required payoffs.
  • Include moving and possession arrangements in the discussion.

4. Share enough for an initial local review

For a Pasadena rental, a Pearland house, or another southeast Houston property, Rickey needs the exact address and current situation. Explain repairs and occupancy without posting financial documents.

  • State your role and any existing agent representation.
  • Describe known deadlines separately from preferred timing.
  • Keep lender discussions moving while a buyer reviews the property.

Questions Homeowners Ask

Does being behind mean I have to sell?+

A sale is one possible path, not a determination of what you must do. Ask your servicer, housing counselor, and attorney about options suited to your situation.

Can I discuss a sale before I know the payoff?+

Yes. State that the amount is unknown. A closing professional and servicer will need to establish transaction-specific figures before you rely on a net-proceeds estimate.

Should I stop talking to the lender while a buyer reviews the house?+

No. Continue addressing the account and notices through the appropriate contacts. A buyer review does not change your obligations or deadlines.

Can I sell without first making repairs?+

You can introduce the property in its present condition. The buyer still needs to evaluate it and agree to written terms.

Do I need to upload a mortgage statement?+

Not for the initial property inquiry. Share basic property facts and keep sensitive account documents for an approved secure process.

Will a sale resolve every debt?+

Do not assume that. Ask your legal and financial advisers what a proposed sale would and would not resolve.
