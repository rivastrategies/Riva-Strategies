Find the right property records in southeast Houston

Find official appraisal and local property-record resources for southeast Houston, including Harris, Galveston, Brazoria, and Fort Bend counties.

By Lone Star Property Solutions · Updated November 9, 2026

The useful starting point

Start with the exact property address and identify its county before selecting an appraisal district. Use official records as a reference, then rely on the appropriate title, survey, or legal professionals for transaction-specific questions.

A city name does not always identify the county

Friendswood spans Harris and Galveston counties. Pearland spans Brazoria, Harris, and Fort Bend counties. Use the specific address and parcel information instead of assuming a county from a mailing label.

  • Match the address with the parcel shown.
  • Check the legal description and account details against records you already hold.
  • Ask the responsible appraisal district when a record or boundary is unclear.

What appraisal information can help you assemble

Appraisal records can be a starting point for identifying the property and gathering descriptive details. An assessed value is not a purchase offer, and an appraisal record is not a substitute for a title commitment or survey.

  • An appraisal-account or parcel reference
  • Property descriptions and existing record links
  • Questions to resolve about additions, parcel identity, or recorded characteristics

Keep each document in its proper role

A tax statement, survey, permit, elevation certificate, and title report serve different purposes. Share what is available without treating one document as proof of every part of the property’s status.

  • Use city or county departments to ask about permits.
  • Use the appropriate appraisal district for appraisal-record questions.
  • Use qualified professionals for ownership, boundaries, and transaction requirements.
