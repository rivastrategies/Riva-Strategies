A useful first package

Bring the facts. Flag the unknowns.

Use this worksheet to prepare a southeast Houston property opportunity. Start with what you have. You do not need to order new reports or finish a renovation to make an initial inquiry.

Property address
Prepared by / date

1. Identify the property

  • □Full street address, city, ZIP and county if known
  • □Property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, approximate size and year built
  • □Your name, contact details and relationship to the property
Find the correct county property record ↗

2. Describe the condition

  • □Roof, foundation, plumbing, electrical and HVAC: known condition or unknown
  • □Water intrusion, flood history and prior repairs, if known
  • □Additions, unfinished renovation or permit records already available
  • □Current photos of the exterior and key interior spaces
Gather flood and repair records ↗

3. Explain occupancy and access

  • □Owner-occupied, tenant-occupied, vacant or unknown
  • □Whether a showing or walkthrough can be arranged and through whom
  • □Relevant lease or possession timing, without tenant identity documents
  • □Do not include lockbox codes or private account information
Review the rental-property guide ↗

4. Outline the proposed terms

  • □Asking price, or state that you are undecided
  • □Preferred timeline and any firm dates
  • □For agents: representation and listing status
  • □For wholesalers: contractual interest, proposed structure and deadlines
See the deal-partner submission guidance ↗

5. Separate facts from assumptions

  • □Label estimates as estimates and explain their source
  • □Mark unknown items instead of guessing
  • □Share only records and materials you are authorized to provide
  • □Keep account numbers, identity documents and sensitive personal records out of the package
