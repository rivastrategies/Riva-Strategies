Does sending a property inquiry stop a foreclosure?+

No. An inquiry or possible offer does not stop a foreclosure or extend a deadline. Contact your lender, attorney, or a qualified housing counselor promptly.

What should I share about a deadline?+

State the known date and the source of that information. Avoid guessing. Property review, title, required approvals, and contract terms still affect whether a sale is possible.
