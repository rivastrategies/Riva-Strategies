Can I introduce a tenant-occupied property?+

Yes. Disclose the occupancy and current lease arrangement. A possible sale needs to account for the lease, applicable rights, and appropriate access procedures.

What rental information belongs in the first submission?+

Start with occupancy, lease status, condition, and your goals. Do not upload tenant identity documents, bank details, or other sensitive tenant records.
