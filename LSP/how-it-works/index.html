How it works

One conversation. A clearer next step.

Explore a direct sale with a process centered on your southeast Houston property and your goals.

01

Start with the property

Tell us the address, what condition the home is in, and what you’re hoping to do next. Let us know about any important dates, tenants, or other decision-makers.

02

Review the possibilities

We’ll review the information and discuss next steps, which may include a walkthrough. We’ll confirm whether the property fits our current buying criteria.

03

Consider the written terms

If we make an offer, review the price, conditions, proposed timing, and responsibilities. Ask questions, compare your options, and involve your advisers as needed.

04

Coordinate an agreed closing

Once an agreement is in place, the closing process still depends on title, required approvals, and the contract terms. Confirm the details in writing before making plans.

Before you get started

No need to have every answer.

A property address and a way to reach you are a good beginning. You can add what you know about the house, your timeline, and the questions you’d like to discuss.
