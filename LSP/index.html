Do I need to repair or clean out the house first?+

Start by telling us about the house in its current condition. Discuss repairs, belongings, and access concerns with us before deciding to take on work.

How is a potential offer considered?+

Location, condition, anticipated work, and the proposed terms all matter. We’ll review the property information and explain any written offer so you can consider the full picture.

How quickly can a sale happen?+

Timing depends on the property review, title, required approvals, and an agreement between the parties. Any closing date needs to be confirmed in the written terms.

What if my house is already listed?+

Let us know and include your agent in the conversation. Your existing listing agreement and representation should be considered before moving forward.

Am I committing to sell by reaching out?+

Reaching out starts a conversation. You can ask questions and review a written offer before deciding whether to enter into an agreement.

Do you consider properties outside the focus areas?+

Send us the address. We’ll confirm whether the location and property fit our current buying criteria.
