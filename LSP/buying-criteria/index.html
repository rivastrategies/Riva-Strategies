Residential acquisition opportunities

What makes a property worth a closer look?

Location, the home’s current condition, and the proposed terms all matter. Share the facts you know so the conversation can start in the right place.

01

Location

Southeast Houston is the focus. Include the exact street address, city, and ZIP; neighborhood or subdivision names alone are not enough.

02

Property & condition

Identify the property type, known repairs, unfinished work, and any damage history. Existing photos and repair estimates help explain the scope.

03

Occupancy & access

Let us know whether the home is owner-occupied, tenant-occupied, or vacant. Explain how an authorized walkthrough could be arranged.

04

Ownership & your role

Tell us whether you are the owner, an agent, a wholesaler, or another authorized contact. Identify other decision-makers and agreements that may affect a sale.

05

Asking terms

Share an asking price when known, along with the timing and conditions you would like considered. An estimate is not a substitute for agreed written terms.

06

Supporting records

Available surveys, permits, elevation certificates, lease information, and repair records can inform the review. Submit only material you are authorized to share.

Unknown is an acceptable starting point.

You do not need a renovation budget, a new inspection, or a completed cleanout to introduce a property. Mark uncertain details clearly. Avoid sharing gate codes, tenant identity documents, financial account numbers, or other sensitive records in the initial form.

Understand the process ↗
Is there a published price range?+

No fixed purchase-price range is published here. Share the asking price if known, along with the property details. Any decision depends on an individual review.

Does every submission receive an offer?+

No. A submission gives us a starting point to evaluate fit. We may need additional information, a walkthrough, or professional review, and some opportunities will not fit.

What if the property is not a single-family house?+

Identify the property type in the form. Homes are the focus; other residential opportunities can be submitted for an individual fit review without an assurance of purchase.
