Does an elevation certificate replace a property inspection?+

No. An elevation certificate, available permit records, flood information, and an inspection serve different purposes. Share relevant documents you already have, and use qualified professionals for property-specific assessments.

Can an agent or deal partner submit a property here?+

Yes. Include your role, representation or contractual interest, asking terms, and information you are authorized to share. Any fees, assignments, and transaction conditions require separate review and agreement.
