What to know locally

Unfinished work. A complete picture.

Pasadena and South Houston have separate municipal departments. Pasadena’s planning resources include site-plan and floodplain-development applications; South Houston publishes its own building, demolition, site-work, and utility forms. The mailing address alone does not settle which approvals apply.

Before you take on the next project

If you are weighing a renovation against an as-is sale, start by separating work you know is needed from work that has not yet been evaluated. Existing estimates and photos can help explain the scope. You do not need to commission new work just to make an initial submission.

Build a clearer submission

  • Describe additions, garage conversions, and unfinished rooms.
  • Include permits or plans you already have; it is fine to say a record is unavailable.
  • Tell us whether utilities are active and whether the property is occupied.
  • Share known water intrusion, drainage concerns, or previous repairs.

Available records are helpful. Unknown details can be discussed during review.

Property questions

Start with clarity.

Find property records ↗
Can I submit a house with an unfinished addition?+

Yes. Describe what has been completed, what remains unfinished, and any permits or plans you have. A submission starts the review; it does not establish that work is permitted or that the property will be purchased.

Can an agent or deal partner submit a property here?+

Yes. Include your role, representation or contractual interest, asking terms, and information you are authorized to share. Any fees, assignments, and transaction conditions require separate review and agreement.
