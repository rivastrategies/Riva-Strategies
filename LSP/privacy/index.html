Information and property submissions

What you share. How it is used.

Updated November 9, 2026. This page describes the website’s current submission and review features.

This is a private review site. Use fictional property and contact information when testing. Test submissions do not send notifications to Rickey.

Information you choose to provide

The property form collects your name, phone or email, relationship to the property, address, property type, occupancy, condition, asking price if provided, preferred timing, optional bedrooms/bathrooms/size/year, representation or contractual details, access arrangements, notes, and optional document links or uploads. It also records a submission reference, time, source page, and your confirmation that the information may be submitted.

Why this information is collected

These details support review of a potential property purchase and follow-up about the inquiry. Submitting a property does not create a purchase agreement, partnership, agency relationship, or entitlement to compensation.

Storage and access

Submission records and uploaded files are stored in the website’s hosted database and file storage. Files are not placed in a public gallery. Access to the review inbox and attachments requires an authorized signed-in account. In private review mode, each account can see its own test submissions. In the public configuration, designated reviewers can access submitted opportunities.

Contact and delivery

The form saves information to the opportunity inbox. This version does not automatically email or text a copy of a submission. Reviewers may use the contact details you provide to discuss a real inquiry once public submissions are enabled.

Technical information

The hosting service processes information needed to operate and secure the website. Public submission protection uses a salted daily hash of the request IP address for rate limiting; the application does not store the raw IP address in the opportunity record. Signed-in review activity uses an account identifier for access control.

Temporary browser storage and inquiry sources

The address entered on the homepage is kept briefly in this browser tab to carry it into the property form, then removed. It is not added to the page URL. The site also keeps the landing-page path, referring website hostname and basic campaign labels in this tab for up to 30 minutes. When you submit a property, those source details accompany the inquiry to help assess where opportunities originate. Referrer queries and browsing history are not collected. Reviewers can add internal notes, priority and a follow-up date to an opportunity.

Proposal comparison worksheet

Amounts and notes entered into the comparison worksheet stay in page memory in your browser. They are not sent with a property inquiry or saved by the website when you leave. Printing or saving a copy creates a file or printout under your control.

Private review history and calendar files

Saved review changes record the reviewer’s display name or email, the time, and changes to status, priority, or follow-up date. Note changes are recorded as an action without keeping previous note text in the activity log. Authorized reviewers may print a private property brief. A downloaded follow-up calendar file includes the reference number, date, and a protected page link; it excludes contact details and internal notes. Importing it into a calendar is a separate action and does not create an ongoing sync.

Measurement

This version does not load Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, advertising pixels, or social integrations. Basic interaction events are queued in browser memory for later measurement setup and are not transmitted to an analytics service. Events do not include the property address, name, phone, email, notes, or uploaded files.

Share only what is needed

Do not upload identity documents, financial account records, passwords, lockbox codes, or private tenant information. Share property materials only when you have authority to do so. External document links remain subject to the access settings and policies of their hosting service.

Retention, corrections, and removal requests

Records remain in the application until removed by an authorized operator. To ask about a real property inquiry or request correction or removal, contact Rickey@lspstexas.com and include the submission reference. For private website testing, contact jordan@rivastrategies.com. Hosting providers may maintain their own operational logs or backups.

Other websites

Official property resources, document links, telephone links, and email links open outside services. Those services handle information under their own policies.
