Before You Decide

Practical Questions. Direct Answers.

Does Lone Star review distressed properties?+

Yes. Introduce the property in its current condition and explain any known deadline, occupancy, or ownership constraint. Each possible purchase requires individual review.

Should I wait until every issue is resolved to reach out?+

You can start with the facts you know. Keep working with your attorney, servicer, housing counselor, or other appropriate advisers while a buyer reviews the property.

Will an inquiry stop foreclosure or a tax sale?+

No. A submission, conversation, or possible offer does not stop a sale or extend a deadline. Seek prompt case-specific help.

What if bankruptcy or probate affects the property?+

Ask your attorney who can authorize a sale and what approvals are required before making commitments. A buyer does not determine your legal authority.

Which southeast Houston communities can submit a property?+

The focus includes Pasadena, South Houston, Deer Park, La Porte, Clear Lake, Webster, Friendswood, Pearland, League City, and Dickinson. Send the full address for a fit review.

What information should I keep out of the form?+

Do not upload IDs, banking or loan-account details, private court filings, tenant records, or access codes. Share basic property information and use an approved secure channel for sensitive records.
