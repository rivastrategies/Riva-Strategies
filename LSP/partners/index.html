A useful introduction

Less back-and-forth.
A clearer first review.

The best starting package separates facts from estimates. A property address, asking terms, current photos, and a clear description of condition give the conversation substance.

If you are sharing comparable sales, repair estimates, or a resale projection, label assumptions and provide their basis. Those materials support review; they do not replace independent evaluation.

Include in your submission

  • Full address and property type
  • Your role and authority to share the opportunity
  • Asking price, relevant dates, and proposed terms
  • Condition, occupancy, and access arrangements
  • Photos and an optional document or listing link
01

Agents

Introduce a listed or off-market property you are authorized to present. Include representation, showing instructions, and any relevant seller timing.

02

Wholesalers

Describe your contractual interest and proposed transaction. Make deadlines, assignment terms, and assumptions clear from the start.

03

Property contacts

If you are helping an owner or identifying an opportunity, explain the connection. Share only information you have permission to provide.

After your introduction

A reference. A review. A conversation.

Your package is saved with a reference number. Review starts with the property facts, your role, and the proposed terms. Missing records, access arrangements, or deadlines may need clarification before a walkthrough or proposal can be considered.

Before sending the deal

Clear roles.
Clear expectations.

Can I submit a listed property?+

Yes. Identify the listing and your relationship to it. Existing representation, listing agreements, and access arrangements must be respected.

Can I submit a property under contract?+

You can introduce the opportunity and clearly describe your contractual interest, asking terms, deadlines, and any proposed assignment. Acceptance and transaction structure require separate review.

Does submitting establish a fee or partnership?+

No. An introduction does not create an agency relationship, partnership, purchase commitment, or right to a fee. Any compensation or transaction terms need to be reviewed and agreed separately.

Is this a capital-investment offering?+

This page is for bringing property acquisition opportunities. It does not offer securities, investment returns, or a program for contributing investment capital.
