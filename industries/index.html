Straight Answers

Industry fit, without the sales fog.

What industries does Riva Strategies serve?

Riva serves ten core industry groups: hospitality and food service; local and field services; professional services; retail and e-commerce; real estate and development; healthcare and medical; technology and software; manufacturing and logistics; financial services; and non-profit and education.

Does Riva only work with hospitality businesses?

No. Hospitality is served through Riva Hospitality Partners, while service companies, professional firms, creators and cross-industry organizations enter through the division or team best aligned with their market and constraint.

How is an industry engagement customized?

Riva evaluates customer behavior, acquisition channels, sales cycles, margins, capacity, retention, compliance, technology and ownership. The resulting plan uses only the capabilities required to address the diagnosed constraint.

Is Riva a marketing agency?

No. Marketing may be part of an engagement, but Riva also works across strategy, technology, AI, automation, sales systems, operating processes, reporting, reputation and business development.

Where does an engagement begin?

Most engagements begin with a Digital Visibility Audit when the immediate question is market presence, or a Growth Systems Audit when the constraint may span acquisition, conversion, retention, technology and operations.

What if an organization spans more than one industry?

Riva can structure cross-division work when a company operates across markets or when the problem touches multiple departments. The engagement is organized around the business system—not forced into a single category.
