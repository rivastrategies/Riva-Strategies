Led by Rick Goodman, Goodman Environmental brings together analyzer systems, project engineering, commissioning, and hands-on industrial service experience.

RICK GOODMAN · PRESIDENT

Experience Across The Life Of A System.

Rick has worked in analyzer systems and industrial field service for more than 30 years. His career spans designing and managing CEMS projects, commissioning equipment, programming controls and DAHS software, and helping customers solve problems in the field.

He established Goodman Environmental Inc. in April 2022. Now based in League City, Texas, Rick combines hands-on technical work with service planning, parts sourcing, customer training, and consulting on equipment modernization.

His background includes work at Cemtek KVB-Enertec, Babcock & Wilcox, Granite Services International / GE Energy Services, KVB-Enertec / GE Energy, and Measurementation.
