Clear commercial boundaries

Responsibility without unlimited scope.

Management fees cover the normal recurring responsibility of the selected level. They do not create unlimited production, unlimited revisions, unlimited access, or responsibility for every marketing-related activity.

Infrastructure first

Required systems must be operational and meet Riva standards. Remediation, migration, reconstruction, and major development are scoped separately.

Projects stay separate

New websites, major redesigns, new brands, new locations, complex automations, major CRM work, photography, video, PR, and abnormal production requirements may require a separate project.

Third-party costs stay visible

Advertising spend, software, CallRail, CRM, email and SMS usage, hosting, premium tools, production vendors, directory fees, and media costs are paid by the client unless expressly stated otherwise.

Complexity can change scope

Multiple brands, websites, locations, markets, territories, or materially expanded advertising programs may require added scope or a higher Management level.
