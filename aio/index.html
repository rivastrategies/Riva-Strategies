What a visibility check means

A dated observation of an agreed set of questions, platforms, and markets. Reports distinguish mentions, citations, and unavailable results. They are not a census of every answer a customer could receive.

What is separately scoped

Website rebuilds, new-page production beyond the agreement, ad spend, paid placements, third-party fees, and additional locations or languages. No unlimited implementation or guaranteed placement is implied.

Prices in USD per month. The $250 rate is a qualifying small-business rate, not a temporary promotion. Qualification, service term, cancellation policy, applicable taxes, and any separately scoped costs must be agreed before billing starts.

Before you decide

Straight answers.

Do I have to do the optimization myself?

No. Choose your plan, confirm your scope, and provide the required access and approvals. Riva handles the agreed optimization and ongoing improvements in both plans. We will contact you when we need business information or approval for a change.

Who is Basic designed for?

Riva does the optimization work for you: we improve agreed website pages, correct business information, implement and validate relevant structured data, and run monthly visibility checks. It is designed as a substantial standalone program for a focused business. Businesses with under $1 million in annual revenue may qualify for the $250/month rate; the breadth of your services, markets, and implementation needs determines the right scope.

Is $250 an introductory discount?

This is not a temporary introductory discount. Basic's list price is $500 per month, with a $250/month rate for qualifying businesses with under $1 million in annual revenue. Riva confirms qualification and any future eligibility review in the agreement before billing begins.

Will my business be guaranteed a place in AI answers?

No. Platforms control their own answers and results. RivaAIO includes active optimization and measurement. Rankings, mentions, citations, traffic, and revenue are not guaranteed. Results can vary by question, location, platform, and date.

Who manages my subscription?

Riva reviews your goals, confirms the scope, and manages the agreed service. QuickBooks handles your authorized monthly payments. Contact Riva directly for service questions or changes to your plan.

How do I start monthly billing?

Submit your request here. Riva reviews the plan, eligibility, scope, and terms with you, then sends a secure QuickBooks recurring-payment invitation. You enter payment details in QuickBooks. The first charge occurs when you accept, followed by automatic monthly payments. Submitting this form does not activate billing; Riva sends the invitation after you agree to your scope and terms.

Can I use my existing website and marketing team?

Yes. Riva carries out agreed optimization in both plans and coordinates with the people who manage your website and business profiles. Your team provides the access and approvals needed. Enhanced+ expands the coverage and implementation capacity.

What about cancellation, timing, and additional fees?

The first payment is charged when you accept the QuickBooks invitation after scope approval, followed by monthly payments. The service term, cancellation process, any taxes or additional charges, and delivery schedule will be confirmed in your agreement before you authorize recurring payment.
