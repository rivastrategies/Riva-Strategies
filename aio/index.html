What a visibility check means

A dated observation of an agreed set of questions, platforms, and markets. Reports distinguish mentions, citations, and unavailable results. They are not a census of every answer a customer could receive.

What is separately scoped

Website rebuilds, new-page production beyond the agreement, ad spend, paid placements, third-party fees, and additional locations or languages. No unlimited implementation or guaranteed placement is implied.

Before you decide

Straight answers.

Is Basic enough for a business under $5 million?

Basic is designed as a substantial, standalone foundation: a baseline, monthly checks, competitor context, and clear recommendations. It can be the right fit for a focused business with someone who can implement the recommendations. Revenue determines eligibility for the $250 rate; complexity, markets, and execution needs determine whether Enhanced+ is a better fit.

Is $250 an introductory discount?

This is not a temporary introductory discount. Basic's list price is $500 per month, with a $250 Small Business Rate for qualifying companies below $5 million in annual revenue. Riva confirms qualification and any future eligibility review in the agreement before billing begins.

Will my business be guaranteed a place in AI answers?

No. Platforms control their own answers and results. RivaAIO proposes a disciplined research and improvement process, not guaranteed rankings, mentions, citations, traffic, or revenue. Results can vary by question, location, platform, and date.

Who manages my subscription?

Riva reviews your goals, confirms the scope, and manages the agreed service. QuickBooks handles your authorized monthly payments. Contact Riva directly for service questions or changes to your plan.

How do I start monthly billing?

Submit your request here. Riva reviews the plan, eligibility, scope, and terms with you, then sends a secure QuickBooks recurring-payment invitation. You enter payment details in QuickBooks. The first charge occurs when you accept, followed by automatic monthly payments. Submitting this form does not activate billing; Riva sends the invitation after you agree to your scope and terms.

Can I use my existing website and marketing team?

Yes. Basic is designed to produce recommendations your team can use. Enhanced+ adds agreed implementation, coordinated with the people who manage your website and business profiles. Access and responsibilities are confirmed before changes are made.

What about cancellation, timing, and additional fees?

The first payment is charged when you accept the QuickBooks invitation after scope approval, followed by monthly payments. The service term, cancellation process, any taxes or additional charges, and delivery schedule will be confirmed in your agreement before you authorize recurring payment.
