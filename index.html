What Riva does, in three moves

Attention from search, paid media, and social channels only matters once it becomes a call, form, or message. Diagnose finds where that chain breaks, Build gives it the lead-generation infrastructure to run, and Run and optimize moves qualified leads toward closed revenue.

Diagnose

Business and growth strategy

Before writing a marketing plan, Riva looks inside the business for the real constraint: a positioning problem, a broken lead process, an unattributed channel, or a gap between departments no single tactic fixes. That diagnosis sets the business growth strategy and priority order before any system gets built.

Brand and market position

Where a business competes and the message it leads with are engineered deliberately, not assumed. Riva sets that brand position before any infrastructure gets built to present it to buyers.

Build

Digital infrastructure, AI, and automation

The technical backbone the strategy calls for: SEO-centered website development, technical SEO, local SEO, entity architecture, Google Business Profile optimization, and AI search visibility. CRM automation, lead routing, and conversion tracking turn a visitor into a qualified lead the sales team sees in time.

Internal operating systems

The lead capture, routing, and follow-up processes that let a growth function run every day without depending on memory, reviewed and adjusted as the business changes.

Run and optimize

Demand generation and channel operations

Paid search and paid social management built around commercial intent, plus organic and local SEO content, social media management, and reputation management, run directly so every channel feeds one pipeline.

Analytics, attribution, and optimization

Analytics, revenue attribution, and call tracking tied back to the CRM, tested and adjusted on a regular schedule, and used to move spend toward what is actually producing revenue.

What operating the system looks like week to week

Not a one-time launch. This is the operating rhythm underneath Diagnose, Build, and Run and optimize, the work a business can see and inherit.

  • Baseline and architecture.Before anything launches, Riva documents where search, entity, and site architecture stand: what ranks, what the structured data and content actually say about the business, and where the technical foundation is holding growth back.
  • Campaign and content operation.Paid campaigns and content run on a working calendar: what publishes, spends, or gets paused is decided every week against what the CRM shows.
  • Routing and follow-up.Every lead lands in a defined stage, routed to a specific owner, and followed up on a schedule Riva can show, not one anyone has to remember.
  • Tracking and reconciliation.Calls, forms, and messages are tracked to their source channel and reconciled through the CRM from first contact to closed revenue, so spend follows what converts.
  • Experiments and adjustment.What is tested, what wins, and what gets cut are recorded and reviewed on a set cadence, so the system keeps improving instead of running on autopilot.

What running the system actually changes

Not more marketing activity. A growth system a business can see, measure, and keep after the first campaign ends.

  • Qualified demand.More of the right buyers reaching a salesperson, not just more traffic.
  • Faster, accountable response.Follow-up built into the process and assigned to someone, not left to memory.
  • Less leakage.Fewer leads go cold between marketing and sales.
  • Clear attribution.Spend moves toward the channels actually producing revenue, not just leads.
  • Stronger close path.Website, advertising, and sales process built to work together.
  • Continuous optimization.The system keeps improving through that same review rhythm, long after the first campaign ends.

The website, the CRM, the tracking, and the accounts belong to the business, not to Riva. If a direct engagement ends, the infrastructure stays.
