Marine Glass FAQs

Do you replace boat windshields?+

Yes, for many boats and watercraft. Availability depends on the windshield design, manufacturer and whether a suitable replacement or fabrication path exists.

Can you work at a marina?+

Mobile marine service may be possible when the marina authorizes access and the work area is safe and suitable.

What should I send for a marine glass quote?+

Send the boat make, model and year, photos of the full windshield system, close-ups of the damage and mounting hardware, and the boat’s location.
