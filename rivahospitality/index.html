Common Questions

What owners ask us.

What does Riva Hospitality Partners do?

We're a growth partner for restaurants, bars, hotels, resorts, marinas, and private clubs. We evaluate five revenue levers together, visibility, retention, reputation, revenue channels, and systems, and build the strategy, technology, and execution needed to move them, rather than running isolated marketing campaigns.

What areas do you serve?

Hospitality operators across the Gulf Coast and Southern US, including Houston, Galveston, Sugar Land, and Pearland, with continued expansion across the region.

How is this different from a marketing agency?

We diagnose the actual bottleneck constraining growth first, whether that's visibility, retention, offer structure, or operations, before recommending a single tactic. Every engagement is measured against revenue, retention, and enterprise value, not campaign metrics.

Who is behind Riva Hospitality Partners?

Riva Hospitality Partners is powered by Riva Strategies, a multidisciplinary team specializing in marketing, AI and automation, SEO, paid media, analytics, business systems, and creative production.

What is the RHP Growth Audit?

A clear assessment and roadmap to more revenue. We evaluate an operator's visibility, reviews, revenue channels, guest experience, operations, and profitability to identify opportunities and prioritize what actually moves the needle.
