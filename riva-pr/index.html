The RivaPR standard

Credibility before volume.

Riva will not turn unsupported claims into news. The strongest release is specific, attributable, approved by the responsible parties, and connected to a page or action that helps the reader verify what comes next.

When the announcement is not ready, Riva will identify the missing facts or supporting material before distribution.

Questions before you start

Know what the release can and cannot do.

What is included in RivaPR?

Story development, fact verification, press release writing, publication support, wire distribution, amplification planning, and reporting for one release.

Does distribution guarantee news coverage?

No. Distribution can increase discoverability and create a durable public record, but it does not guarantee independent editorial coverage, interviews, backlinks, rankings, or sales.

What does Riva need to begin?

The announcement, the people and organizations involved, the facts that can be verified, approved quotes, supporting links or files, and the intended audience.

Can RivaPR support a broader campaign?

Yes. Riva can connect the release to website content, social channels, business profiles, email, paid media, or a larger growth system when the strategy supports it. Those services are scoped separately.
