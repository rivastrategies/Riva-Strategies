The RivaPR standard

Credibility before volume.

Riva will not turn unsupported claims into news or confuse paid distribution with independent coverage. Strong public relations is specific, attributable, approved by the responsible parties, and connected to evidence a reader can verify.

When the story is not ready, RivaPR identifies the missing facts, approvals, source material, or operating decision before taking it public.

Questions before you start

Know what the release can and cannot do.

What does RivaPR do?

RivaPR provides public relations strategy, narrative development, press releases, media relations, executive communications, publication and distribution, amplification, monitoring, and reporting.

Does distribution guarantee news coverage?

No. Distribution can increase discoverability and create a durable public record, but it does not guarantee independent editorial coverage, interviews, backlinks, rankings, or sales.

What does Riva need to begin?

The business objective, intended audience, relevant people and organizations, facts that can be verified, existing messages, supporting links or files, approval owners, and any timing or risk constraints.

Can RivaPR work with an internal marketing team?

Yes. RivaPR can lead the public relations function or work as a specialist alongside internal marketing, executive leadership, legal counsel, subject-matter experts, and other outside partners.
